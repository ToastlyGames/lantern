Cosmetic NFTs setup

For the Lanternhold owner (Season 1). Players can turn cosmetics they earned in the game (the Jack-o'-Lantern, the Reaper's Scythe, and every cosmetic added later) into ERC-1155 NFTs on Robinhood Chain, tradeable on OpenSea. A minted cosmetic leaves the hero; whoever's linked wallet holds the NFT can wear it. Open this page in your wallet app's browser. The wallet you deploy with owns the collection and receives royalties (5%).

Not connected.

1. Deploy the collection

Creates the Lanternhold Cosmetics contract. Only vouchers the game signs can mint, one per cosmetic a hero earned. You can't mint any yourself.

2. Switch it on (Season 1)

Run supabase/cosmetics-nft.sql in Supabase once. Then in Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables: COSMETICS_CONTRACT = the collection's address. Players can wear cosmetic NFTs they hold from then on. When Season 1 starts, add COSMETICS_NFT = 1 and redeploy: the Mint as NFT buttons appear in the game. Check /api/health → cosmetics.
