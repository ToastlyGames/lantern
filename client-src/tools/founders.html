Founder's Lantern setup

For the Lanternhold owner. Open this page in your wallet app's browser (MetaMask, Rabby…) on Robinhood Chain. The wallet you deploy with becomes the collection's owner and receives royalties (5%).

Not connected.

1. Deploy the collection

Creates the Founder's Lantern NFT contract. Hero numbers 1 to the whitelist size can mint, once each, one per wallet, free (gas only).

2. Open the mint

In Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables: FOUNDERS_CONTRACT = the collection's address, and FOUNDERS_SIGNER_KEY = the signer's private key (mark it Sensitive; if you leave it out, CLAIM_SIGNER_KEY is used). Redeploy, then open /api/health: founders.open should be true and founders.heroNumbers ok.
