Founder's Lantern setup

For the Lanternhold owner. Open this page in your wallet app's browser (MetaMask, Rabby…) on Robinhood Chain. The wallet you deploy with becomes the collection's owner and receives royalties (5%).

Not connected.

1. Deploy the collection

Creates the Founder's Lantern NFT contract: 2,222 in all. Any hero can mint their own number, once, one per wallet, free (gas only). 1,222 are open to every hero first-come; 1,000 are held for the X giveaway winners you add below.

2. Open the mint

In Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables: FOUNDERS_CONTRACT = the collection's address, and FOUNDERS_SIGNER_KEY = the signer's private key (mark it Sensitive; if you leave it out, CLAIM_SIGNER_KEY is used). Redeploy, then open /api/health: founders.mint should be true, and founders.heroNumbers and founders.giveaway ok (run supabase/founders-giveaway.sql once).

4. X giveaway winners

Add the winners by hero name (one per line, or separated by commas). A winner's mint comes from the giveaway pool, so they're sure of a Lantern even once the open pool runs out. Nothing in the game says the pools exist; winners just see "giveaway winner" on their own Founder's Lantern screen.
