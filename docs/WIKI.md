# Lanternhold Wiki

Season 0 (Beta) · build v0.13.4 · updated October 28, 2026

## Welcome

Lanternhold is a pixel-art dungeon MMO for Robinhood Chain. You can play it in your browser, on a phone or a computer, at [lanternhold.fun](https://lanternhold.fun). Nothing to download or install.

The game is in **Season 0 (Beta)**. Every player you meet is a real person, and your progress saves to your account. The token and on-chain items arrive in Season 1.

**How it plays**

1. **Build up at the Hold.** The Hold is a shared town. You farm, fish, chop trees, cook and forge there, alongside everyone in your world.
2. **Delve into the Depths.** Light your lantern and head down through floors of monsters, ore and chests, alone or with up to 3 friends.
3. **Bring the loot home.** Light the Return Flame to get home with everything you found. If you fall, you lose about half of what you were carrying.
4. **Grow stronger.** Better gear takes you deeper, deeper floors drop better loot, and goals reward you along the way.

**At a glance:** 8 skills (Woodcutting, Fishing, Farming, Cooking, Smithing, Mining, Crafting and Magic) plus a combat level, 30 dungeon floors across 6 biomes with a boss each, Hollowmere (a wild land west of the Hold), 87 goals (10 of them secret) and items from Common all the way up to Mythic.

## Getting started

Sign in, choose a name and a look, and you start in the Hold with 40 gold, 20 Wheat Seeds, 3 Oil Flasks and 3 Bread.

**Signing in.** There are no guest accounts. Pick one:

- **Wallet:** any EVM wallet, such as MetaMask. It takes two approvals: connect, then one free signature. Signing is never a transaction, costs no gas and gives no access to your funds.
- **Google:** one tap. You can connect a wallet later from the Hero screen.

You can also link your X account from the Hero screen so friends can see your @handle.

**Your hero.** Names are 3 to 16 characters (letters, numbers or \_). They are unique and can't be changed. You choose a skin tone, hair colour, hair style (short, long, spiky, hood, pigtails, ponytail, bun or bob) and clothes, and you can change your look later at the house in the Hold.

**First things to try**

1. Plant Wheat at the Farm, then cast a line at the lake while it grows.
2. Chop a few logs from a tree, then bake Bread at the Kitchen.
3. Read the Bulletin at the crossroads to see what's new.
4. When you're ready, walk up the stairs into the Delve Gate. Bring Oil Flasks and food.

The goals pill at the top of the screen always suggests a good next step.

**Worlds.** Each world holds up to 50 players. When you sign in, you join the busiest world that has room, or the one your party is in. You can switch any time from Social → Worlds. Trees, chat and items on the ground belong to each world. Your progress, friends and party come with you.

### Woodcutting

Walk up to a tree and tap **Chop**. Each swing has a chance to give you 1 log and **3 XP**.

- At level 1 you swing every 2.6 seconds with a 62% chance per swing. Each level adds 0.5% to your chance (up to 95%) and makes you swing a little faster (down to 1.6 seconds).
- Trees are shared with everyone in your world. Each oak holds 300 logs. When it runs out it falls ("Timber!") and grows back as a full tree after 20 seconds.
- A tree that was only partly cut grows its logs back by itself: 15 seconds after the last swing, it starts adding 1 log every 2 seconds until it's full.
- Woodcutting XP is slow on purpose. Level 30 takes around 4 hours of chopping.
- Logs sell for 2 gold each, but a future skill will use them, so it's worth stocking up.
- **Willow trees** grow all over the Hollowmere moor (Woodcutting 30). Each holds 80 Willow Logs worth 18 gold each and gives **18 XP** per log. A fallen willow grows back in 60 seconds. Watch out: Hollowmere's monsters roam nearby.
- **Blackwood trees** grow in the Wilderness (Woodcutting 15). Each holds 60 Blackwood Logs worth 9 gold each and gives **9 XP** per log, but they take 45 seconds to grow back and you can be attacked while you chop.

### Fishing

Stand at the edge of the lake, or out on the dock, and tap **Fish**. Your line always lands in open water. Wait for the bobber to settle, and when it dips, tap **Hook!** for a guaranteed catch. If you miss the moment, you still have a 32% chance to catch it (plus 0.6% per Fishing level).

- **Pondfin:** Fishing 1, 10 XP each, in the Hold's lake.
- **Gloomfin:** Fishing 15, 30 XP each, only in the **Gloom Pond** in the Wilderness. Cooked, it heals 55.
- **Moonfin:** Fishing 30, 60 XP each, only in the **Moonmere** in Hollowmere. Walk out along its dock to the platform in the middle. Cooked, it heals 85.
- The **Angler's Cape** can only be caught while fishing, and it's extremely rare: about 1 in every 35,000 catches. There's always a chance on every catch.

### Magic

Magic is a combat skill of its own. **Wield a staff** and your attacks become spells: the action button says **Cast**, and every monster you defeat with a staff in hand trains **Magic instead of Fighting** (twice the monster's XP). Put a blade back on and you're training Fighting again. Magic never trains Fighting, and Fighting never trains Magic. Facing an ore vein, your staff works as a pick, so you don't need to swap to mine.

**Spells.** Open Hero (H) while holding a staff and tap **Spellbook** to pick the spell your attack casts. Combat spells need no runes: your staff powers them. Low spells hit low; each new spell hits harder.

### Combat level

Your **combat level** comes from everything you do: **(2 × the higher of Fighting and Magic + all your skill levels) ÷ 10**. Fighting is trained by defeating monsters with a blade, and Magic by defeating them with a staff; whichever is higher counts double. Woodcutting, Fishing, Farming, Cooking, Smithing, Mining, Crafting and Magic each add their own level too. A new hero is combat level 1. Combat level 10 takes something like Fighting (or Magic) 18 with your other skills around 8. Fighting tops out at 99, like every skill, so the highest combat level is 99. Nobody has ever lost a level when the formula changed: heroes keep the combat level they had.

You earn Fighting XP by defeating monsters in the Depths and Hollowmere. Everyone who hits a monster gets its full XP, and in a party **every member in the dungeon** gets the full XP for every kill, even without landing a hit, so fighting together never costs you anything. Each Fighting level needs 22 × level^1.5 XP. Every combat level adds 8 max HP (you start with 60 HP). Your combat level shows next to your name.

## The Depths

The Depths are dungeon floors below the Delve Gate, built fresh every time you go down. On each floor, fight what lives there, find the stairs down, and get home with the loot. Each delve belongs to you alone, or to you and your party.

| Floors | Biome | Monsters | Ore | Boss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 5 | Mossroot Caverns | Slime, Bat, Crawler | Copper | Mother Slime (floor 5) |
| 6 to 10 | Bone Crypts | Skeleton, Wisp, Spider, Bat | Iron, rare Emberite | The Ossuary Knight (floor 10) |
| 11 to 15 | Emberforge Halls | Fire Imp, Magma Golem, Cinder Bat | Coal, Iron, rare Emberite | The Forge Tyrant (floor 15) |
| 16 to 20 | Starfall Hollow | Wraith, Crystal Sentinel, Shardling | Cobalt, Coal, rare Starshard | The Starwarden (floor 20) |
| 21 to 25 | The Drowned Archive | Drowned Scribe, Tide Eel, Cursed Grimoire | Gloamite, Cobalt, Coal | The Drowned Archmage (floor 25) |
| 26 to 30 | The Hollow Throne | Hollow Husk, Voidling, Watcher | Gloamite, Cobalt, rare Starshard | The Hollow King (floor 30) |

**Your lantern.** It starts full and burns oil as you go, faster on deeper floors. Better lanterns burn slower and light up more of the dark. Drink an Oil Flask (+50%) or a Greater Oil Flask (+100%) from a quick slot to top it up. If your oil runs out, a Shade appears every few seconds and hunts you down.

**Getting home.** Hold the Return Flame button (or R) for 2 seconds to go home with everything you found. If you fall in battle, you lose about half of this delve's loot and half of what's in your bag. Your Bank and the gear you're wearing are always safe.

**What you'll find.** Ore glints in the walls (hit it to mine it), chests hide in dead ends (on floors 21 to 30, often a green-glowing **Uncommon Chest**), glowcaps grow on the floor, and the stairs lead down. Each floor is tougher than the last: monsters hit harder and take more hits to beat.

**The lift.** Beat a boss and the lift at the Delve Gate can take you straight to the next area: Mother Slime opens floor 6, the Ossuary Knight floor 11, the Forge Tyrant floor 16, the Starwarden floor 21 and the Drowned Archmage floor 26.

**Boss attacks.** The Forge Tyrant charges, calls fire imps and slams its hammer: a **ring of fire** on the floor shows where it will land, so step out before it fills. The Starwarden **blinks** to the far side of you, then bursts with starlight inside a **cold ring**, and calls wraiths to its side. Both get faster below half health.

The two deepest bosses mix their attacks. **The Drowned Archmage** keeps its distance and throws fans of tide bolts (dodge them, they fly in straight lines), blinks away when you close in, bursts with a **frost nova** inside a cold ring, and calls Cursed Grimoires to defend it. **The Hollow King** walks you down, slams the floor inside a **violet ring**, charges, and tears **rifts** open under your feet (step off the glowing mark). Below half health it also throws a ring of void bolts all around it.

**Uncommon Chests.** On floors 21 to 30 most chests glow green. They hold more gold, ore, gems, runes and seeds than a normal chest, have twice the chance of a lantern or cape, and a small chance (7%) of a **Greater Oil Flask**.

### Rare gear drops

Lanterns and capes can drop from chests (10% for a lantern, 3.5% for a cape), from monsters (0.6% and 0.25%) and from bosses (50% and 25%). Each boss also has a 5% chance to drop its own Legendary cape. Deeper floors and Loot Luck improve the rarity of what you find.

**The two rarest finds in the Depths** are Mythic: **Cinderfang** (damage 46), only from the Forge Tyrant, and the **Starwarden's Helm** (defence 9, +25 max HP, +15% light, +10% Loot Luck), only from the Starwarden. Each has a 1.5% chance per kill, a little better with Loot Luck.

The floor 25 and floor 30 bosses each drop **one staff and one robe**, one Legendary and one Mythic (see Magic): the Drowned Archmage has the Mythic Moonwell Staff and the Legendary Archmage's Tiderobe, and the Hollow King has the Legendary Hollow Scepter and the Mythic Voidwoven Robe.

## The Wilderness

The first expansion to Lanternhold. North of the Hold, past the old stone wall, lies the **Wilderness**: dead grass, ash on the wind, and other heroes who can attack you. Walk through either gap in the wall to cross. The first time, a warning explains the rules and asks you to confirm.

**Wilderness levels.** The further north you go, the higher the level, from **1** just past the wall to **20** at the Old Obelisk at the very top. Your level and the combat levels you can fight show in a red badge while you're there.

**Who can fight whom.** You can attack a hero when you're both in the Wilderness and your combat levels are no further apart than the Wilderness level (the lower of the two spots you're standing in). At level 5, a combat level 20 hero can fight levels 15 to 25. Deeper means more dangerous.

**Fighting.** Walk up to a hero and press **Attack** (or click them). One hit every 0.8 seconds. Damage comes from your blade and cape, and their armor, helmet and cape soak some of it up. Some swings miss. In the Wilderness everyone's name, level and health bar show, whatever your settings.

| Gear | In a fight |
| --- | --- |
| Blades | Rusty 6, Copper 10, Iron 16, Ember 25, Steel 34, Gloamite 40, Cinderfang 46 (base damage). Spells don't work in PvP: a staff fights as a club. |
| Armor | Copper 2, Iron 4, Ember 7, Steel 10, Gloamite 13 defense (robes: Tiderobe 8, Voidwoven 11) |
| Helmets | Copper 1, Iron 2, Ember 4, Steel 5, Gloamite 7, Starwarden's Helm 9 defense (admin hats count as Ember) |
| Capes | Mother's Mantle +15% damage, Pall of the Ossuary +12% damage and 3 defense, Gravecloth Mantle 2 defense |

**Health.** Your health is 60 + 8 per combat level, plus any cape bonus. Eat from your bag with the quick slots (one bite every 1.5 seconds). After 8 seconds out of a fight your health climbs back 1 a second.

**The skull.** Attack someone who hasn't attacked you in the last 2 minutes and a **skull** appears over your head for **20 minutes**. Fighting back never skulls you. Your skull follows you to other worlds.

**Falling.** If you fall in the Wilderness you wake up back in the Hold, healed, and drop your items where you fell: everything in your bag and the gear you wear. Your party gets a message in Party chat.

- **No skull:** you keep your **3 most valuable items**.
- **Skulled:** you keep **nothing** you carry or wear. Your Bank is still untouched.
- Your **Bank** is always safe, and so is your gold. Starter gear (the Rusty Blade, Traveler Garb and Basic Lantern) and bound or goal items are never dropped.
- The winner sees the loot at once and has **one minute** to pick it up before everyone else can. Walk over it (or tap Pick up) to grab it. Loot disappears after 3 minutes.

**The Return Flame.** Hold the flame button (or R) for 2 seconds to go home to the Hold, even in the middle of a fight. A hit interrupts it, so pick your moment. It works up to Wilderness level 20; deeper areas added later will block it.

**No escaping.** Logging out in the middle of a fight (or losing your connection) leaves your hero standing there for 15 seconds, and they can still be defeated. Come back in time and you're right where you were. You can't walk off or teleport out of a fight either, except with the Return Flame.

**What's up there.** The **Gloom Pond** (Gloomfin, Fishing 15), **Blackwood trees** (Woodcutting 15), an old graveyard, and the Old Obelisk at level 20.

**The compass.** The small compass left of Settings always points north, toward the Wilderness.

## Hollowmere

Walk out of the Hold to the west and its green grass and oaks run on into the **Hollow Wood**. Dusk falls a little more with every step, until the wood opens onto **Hollowmere**: a vast twilight moor, nearly twenty times the size of the Hold (96 by 120 tiles), with roads lit by lamp posts. Fireflies drift over the grass, willows hang over the water, and in the far north-west a great horned skull breathes violet fire.

**Monsters.** Hollowmere's monsters live in dens and attack if you get too close. Back off far enough and they give up and wander home. Each shows its level, from **2 to 28**:

| Area | Monsters | Levels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The edge of the Hollow Wood | Carrion Raven | 2 to 4 |
| The meadows (north, past the wood) | Moor Wolf, Bristleboar | 3 to 8 |
| The marsh (west, around the bog pools) | Bogling, Mirewisp | 9 to 14 |
| The fens (east of the Moonmere) | Gloomstag | 12 to 16 |
| The deepwood (south-east, among the dead trees) | Deepwood Treant | 15 to 20 |
| The Mossy Barrows (far south-west) | **Mossy Giant** | 22 to 28 |

Defeating them trains Fighting (or Magic with a staff) and drops gold and runes. Treants drop logs and Blackwood Logs; mossy giants drop gold, logs, glowcap spores and sometimes an Emerald. There's no PvP in Hollowmere. When a monster comes for you, the action button fights, even if you're next to a willow or the water.

**Mordecai the Reaper** stands by the road where the Hollow Wood ends, among candle-lit pumpkins and old graves. Talk to him for the Halloween quest (see Quests).

**Falling in Hollowmere** costs nothing: you wake up in the Hold with everything you had. Your health is your full health here and in the Hold. Only the Wilderness, north of the wall, lowers it: there your max health is your level plus your cape, the same rules for everyone.

**The Moonmere.** A great moonlit lake in the middle of the moor, far bigger than the Hold's, with a dock two planks wide out to a big 5-by-5 fishing platform in the middle. It's the only place to catch **Moonfin** (Fishing 30).

**The Abyssal Maw.** The entrance to Lanternhold's raids, in the far north-west: a vast horned skull set into the cliffs, with a portal burning in its jaw. The plaza in front of the Maw is **safe**: no monster comes near it. A **Bank chest** stands by the campfire on the plaza, so you can bank your loot without walking home.

**Raids (Season 1).** Raids arrive in Season 1, and every raid is a **world event for the whole server**:

- **One raid every hour.** When a raid goes live, the whole game hears about it.
- **Every world, together.** Players from every world can join the same raid at the same time.
- **The raid queue.** Join the queue at the Maw. A raid begins once at least **10 players** are queued.

For now, step up to the Maw and it explains all of this.

## Bag, Bank and trading

Your **bag** goes everywhere with you and is at risk in the Depths. Your **Bank** is always safe.

**Bag (B).** Shows your gold and everything you're carrying. Tap an item to see what it does, equip it, put it in a quick slot or drop it. In the Depths, it also lists what you've found on this delve.

**Quick slots.** 5 slots for food and Oil Flasks that you can use with one tap (or keys 1 to 5) in the Depths.

**Bank.** Each Bank slot holds any amount of one item. You start with 16 slots and can grow to 72:

| How | Extra slots |
| --- | --- |
| Defeat Mother Slime | 4 |
| Defeat the Ossuary Knight | 6 |
| Reach Farming, Fishing, Cooking or Smithing 15 | 2 each |
| Reach combat level 10 | 2 |
| Complete 25 goals | 4 |
| Buy expansions: 8 purchases of 4 slots, for 250, 750, 2,000, 5,000, 10,000, 20,000, 40,000 and 75,000 gold | 32 |

**Bank everything but supplies** stores your loot but keeps Oil Flasks and food in your bag for the next delve. The Forge, Kitchen and Oil Press can use items straight from your Bank.

**Trading with players.** In the Hold, right-click a player and choose **Trade** (on a computer), or open Social → Nearby and tap **Trade** next to them (on a phone). Once they accept, you both see a trade window: add items from your bag and some gold, and watch their offer update live. Both of you must tap **Accept trade**, and any change to either offer resets both accepts, so check what you're getting first. When both accept, the items move for both of you at the same moment. One side can be empty, which makes it a gift (the window says so clearly), but a trade needs at least one item or some gold. Goal Relics and the admin party hats can't be traded. Trades only happen in the Hold, never in the Depths.

**Tallow the trader.** Sell anything in your bag or Bank for gold (gear you're wearing isn't listed). He sells Wheat Seeds (5 gold), Lampseed (30), Oil Flasks (45), Bread (20), Moonpetal Seeds (90) and Gale, Stone, Tide and Ember Runes (5, 8, 14 and 18).

**Items on the ground**

- **Monster loot in a party:** everyone who hit a monster at least once gets their own roll at its drops, bosses included. Your loot is yours alone: only you see it and pick it up.
- **Chests, ore and glowcaps in a party:** what they drop appears for the whole party, and whoever walks over it first gets it.
- **Dropping from your bag:** the item lands at your feet and you can tap **Pick up** to take it back straight away. After 30 seconds, other players nearby can see it and pick it up. It disappears after 3 minutes.
- **Dropping in a party dungeon:** your party sees the item right away and can pick it up straight away. Handy for sharing food or oil mid-delve.
- The server decides who gets each item, so nothing is ever picked up twice.

## Playing together

Everyone in your world shares the Hold. You see each other walk, fish, chop and chat in real time, and you can team up for the Depths.

**Chat.** Tap the chat button (or press T). The **World** tab talks to everyone on the same map; the **Party** tab talks to your party only, wherever they are (starting a World message with **/p** also sends it to your party). A red badge shows new messages on the tab you're not looking at. Messages appear above the speaker's head. Chat is filtered and rate-limited to stop spam, and it starts fresh each time you sign in: nothing is stored.

**World feed.** Big moments scroll across the top of the screen for everyone: rare finds, boss kills, new combat levels and deep floors reached.

**Social (top right)**

- **Friends:** add players by hero name, accept requests, see who's online and where, and join a friend's world.
- **Party:** up to 4 players. The leader opens the Delve Gate and everyone is invited into the same dungeon. Leaders can pass on leadership or remove members.
- **Nearby:** everyone on your map, with quick Add friend and Invite buttons.
- **Worlds:** every world and how many players are in it. Switch to one with room, or open a new one.

**Party dungeons.** The whole party shares one dungeon. If the leader leaves, another member takes over without a break. You see each other's swings, damage numbers, mining and chest opening. Every party member in the dungeon gets the full combat XP for every kill. Everyone who hits a monster at least once also gets their own roll at its loot.

**Name tags.** Names and combat levels show over everyone's heads (turn them off in Settings → Player names). The colour of the level compares them to you:

| Colour | Their combat level |
| --- | --- |
| Green | The same as yours or lower |
| Yellow | 1 to 5 levels higher |
| Red | 6 or more levels higher |

**Admins** have a gold crown before their name, and their names always show. Lantern and Kush are the admins in Season 0.

**One device at a time.** Signing in on a second device signs you out on the first.

## Your account and staying safe

Your hero saves to your account automatically, so you can pick up where you left off on any device by signing in the same way. You sign back in exactly where you logged out; if you logged out in the Depths, you come back at the entrance you went in by.

- **Saving:** your progress, settings, goals and Bank save to the server as you play. The server checks every save, so nobody can give themselves impossible gold, XP or items.
- **One hero per account,** and one device online at a time.
- **Always up to date:** if an update comes out while you're playing, the game tells you and reloads by itself, so everyone in a world plays by the same rules.

## FAQ

**What do I lose if I fall in the Wilderness?**
Your bag and the gear you wear, dropped where you fell. Without a skull you keep your 3 most valuable items; with a skull, nothing. Your Bank and gold are always safe.

**What do I lose if I fall in the Depths?**
About half of this delve's loot and half of your bag. Your Bank and the gear you're wearing are always safe.

**How do I get more oil?**
Grow Lampseed at the Farm (Farming 3) and press it at the Oil Press, or buy Oil Flasks from Tallow. From Farming 30, add Moonpetal to press a Greater Oil Flask, which fills twice as much.

**How do I train Magic?**
Carve an Apprentice Staff at the Jeweler's Bench (10 logs), wield it, and fight. Every kill with a staff trains Magic. Pick stronger spells from the Spellbook on the Hero screen as you level.

**How do I get more farm plots?**
Level up Farming (new plots open at levels 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60) or buy them with gold from the Farm screen.

**Why can't I see my friend?**
You might be in different worlds. Use Social → Friends → Join world, or pick their world from Social → Worlds.

**Why can't I see anyone's name?**
Player names are on by default. Turn them off in Settings → Player names.

**Can I play on my phone?**
Yes. Lanternhold is built for phones first, upright or sideways.

**Does signing in cost anything?**
No. Wallet sign-in is a free signature, not a transaction.

**Is my progress saved if I close the game?**
Yes. Your hero saves to your account as you play, and crops keep growing while you're away.
