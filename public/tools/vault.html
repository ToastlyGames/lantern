Rewards vault setup

For the Lanternhold owner. Open this page in your wallet app’s browser (MetaMask, Rabby…) with the wallet that holds the rewards tokens, on Robinhood Chain.

Not connected.

1. Deploy the vault

Creates the rewards vault contract. You’ll be its owner (you can pause it and change the signer or daily cap).

2. Fund it

Sends tokens from this wallet into the vault (50,000,000 = 5% of supply).

3. Turn it on

In Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables: CLAIM_CONTRACT = the vault address, CLAIM_SIGNER_KEY = the signer’s private key (mark it Sensitive), and REWARDS_ON = 1. Then redeploy and open /api/health: rewards.claims should be true.
