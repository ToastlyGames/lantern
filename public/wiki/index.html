Season 0 (Beta) · build v0.8.0 · updated October 24, 2026

Welcome#

Lanternhold is a pixel-art dungeon MMO for Robinhood Chain. You can play it in your browser, on a phone or a computer, at lanternhold.fun. Nothing to download or install.

The game is in Season 0 (Beta). Every player you meet is a real person, and your progress saves to your account. The token and on-chain items arrive in Season 1.

How it plays

  1. Build up at the Hold. The Hold is a shared town. You farm, fish, chop trees, cook and forge there, alongside everyone in your world.
  2. Delve into the Depths. Light your lantern and head down through floors of monsters, ore and chests, alone or with up to 3 friends.
  3. Bring the loot home. Light the Return Flame to get home with everything you found. If you fall, you lose about half of what you were carrying.
  4. Grow stronger. Better gear takes you deeper, deeper floors drop better loot, and goals reward you along the way.

At a glance: 5 skills (Woodcutting, Fishing, Farming, Cooking and Smithing) plus a combat level, 2 dungeon biomes with a boss each, 48 goals (9 of them secret) and items from Common all the way up to Mythic.

Getting started#

Sign in, choose a name and a look, and you start in the Hold with 40 gold, 20 Wheat Seeds, 3 Oil Flasks and 3 Bread.

Signing in. There are no guest accounts. Pick one:

  • Wallet: any EVM wallet, such as MetaMask. It takes two approvals: connect, then one free signature. Signing is never a transaction, costs no gas and gives no access to your funds.
  • Google: one tap. You can connect a wallet later from the Hero screen.

You can also link your X account from the Hero screen so friends can see your @handle.

Your hero. Names are 3 to 16 characters (letters, numbers or _). They are unique and can't be changed. You choose a skin tone, hair colour, hair style (short, long, spiky or hood) and clothes, and you can change your look later at the house in the Hold.

First things to try

  1. Plant Wheat at the Farm, then cast a line at the lake while it grows.
  2. Chop a few logs from a tree, then bake Bread at the Kitchen.
  3. Read the Bulletin at the crossroads to see what's new.
  4. When you're ready, walk up the stairs into the Delve Gate. Bring Oil Flasks and food.

The goals pill at the top of the screen always suggests a good next step.

Worlds. Each world holds up to 50 players. When you sign in, you join the busiest world that has room, or the one your party is in. You can switch any time from Social → Worlds. Trees, chat and items on the ground belong to each world. Your progress, friends and party come with you.

Woodcutting#

Walk up to a tree and tap Chop. Each swing has a chance to give you 1 log and 3 XP.

  • At level 1 you swing every 2.6 seconds with a 62% chance per swing. Each level adds 0.5% to your chance (up to 95%) and makes you swing a little faster (down to 1.6 seconds).
  • Trees are shared with everyone in your world. Each oak holds 300 logs. When it runs out it falls ("Timber!") and grows back as a full tree after 20 seconds.
  • A tree that was only partly cut grows its logs back by itself: 15 seconds after the last swing, it starts adding 1 log every 2 seconds until it's full.
  • Woodcutting XP is slow on purpose. Level 30 takes around 4 hours of chopping.
  • Logs sell for 2 gold each, but a future skill will use them, so it's worth stocking up.

Fishing#

Stand at the edge of the lake, or out on the dock, and tap Fish. Your line always lands in open water. Wait for the bobber to settle, and when it dips, tap Hook! for a guaranteed catch. If you miss the moment, you still have a 32% chance to catch it (plus 0.6% per Fishing level).

  • Pondfin: Fishing 1, 10 XP each. More fish, each needing a higher level, will come with new waters.
  • The rare Angler's Cape can only be caught while fishing: about 1 in every 300 catches.

Combat level#

You earn combat XP by defeating monsters. Everyone who hits a monster gets its full XP, so fighting together never costs you anything. Each level needs 22 × level^1.5 XP and adds 8 max HP (you start with 60 HP). Your combat level shows next to your name.

The Depths#

The Depths are dungeon floors below the Delve Gate, built fresh every time you go down. On each floor, fight what lives there, find the stairs down, and get home with the loot. Each delve belongs to you alone, or to you and your party.

FloorsBiomeMonstersOreBoss

Your lantern. It starts full and burns oil as you go, faster on deeper floors. Better lanterns burn slower and light up more of the dark. Drink an Oil Flask (+50%) from a quick slot to top it up. If your oil runs out, a Shade appears every few seconds and hunts you down.

Getting home. Hold the Return Flame button (or R) for 2 seconds to go home with everything you found. If you fall in battle, you lose about half of this delve's loot and half of what's in your bag. Your Bank and the gear you're wearing are always safe.

What you'll find. Ore glints in the walls (hit it to mine it), chests hide in dead ends, glowcaps grow on the floor, and the stairs lead down. Each floor is tougher than the last: monsters hit harder and take more hits to beat.

The lift. After you beat Mother Slime, the lift at the Delve Gate can take you straight to floor 6.

Rare gear drops#

Lanterns and capes can drop from chests (10% for a lantern, 3.5% for a cape), from monsters (0.6% and 0.25%) and from bosses (50% and 25%). Each boss also has a 5% chance to drop its own Legendary cape. Deeper floors and Loot Luck improve the rarity of what you find.

Relics and special items#

  • Boss relics: the Slime Crown (from Mother Slime) and the Knight's Sigil (from the Ossuary Knight). In Season 1, relics can be minted on-chain and traded.
  • Goal Relics: the Starlit Lantern and Wayfinder's Mantle can't be found anywhere else. Every goal you complete has a tiny chance to award one (see Goals). They can't be sold, traded or dropped.
  • Admin party hats: the Blue Party Hat and Green Party Hat belong to the two admins. They are one of a kind and can never drop, be sold or be traded.

Materials and supplies#

  • For the Forge: Copper, Iron and Emberite Ore, Cave Gems, Slime Gel and Old Bone.
  • From the Farm: Wheat, Lampseed and Glowcap.
  • For your lantern: Oil Flasks.
  • Healing food: Bread, Cooked Pondfin and Glowcap Stew.
  • Logs: being stockpiled for a skill that's on the way.

Bag, Bank and trading#

Your bag goes everywhere with you and is at risk in the Depths. Your Bank is always safe.

Bag (B). Shows your gold and everything you're carrying. Tap an item to see what it does, equip it, put it in a quick slot or drop it. In the Depths, it also lists what you've found on this delve.

Quick slots. 5 slots for food and Oil Flasks that you can use with one tap (or keys 1 to 5) in the Depths.

Bank. Each Bank slot holds any amount of one item. You start with 16 slots and can grow to 72:

HowExtra slots
Defeat Mother Slime4
Defeat the Ossuary Knight6
Reach Farming, Fishing, Cooking or Smithing 152 each
Reach combat level 102
Complete 25 goals4
Buy expansions: 8 purchases of 4 slots, for 250, 750, 2,000, 5,000, 10,000, 20,000, 40,000 and 75,000 gold32

Bank everything but supplies stores your loot but keeps Oil Flasks and food in your bag for the next delve. The Forge, Kitchen and Oil Press can use items straight from your Bank.

Tallow the trader. Sell anything in your bag or Bank for gold (gear you're wearing isn't listed). He sells Wheat Seeds (5 gold), Lampseed (30), Oil Flasks (45) and Bread (20).

Items on the ground

  • Party loot: in a party dungeon, everything that drops appears for the whole party. Whoever walks over it first gets it.
  • Dropping from your bag: the item lands at your feet and you can tap Pick up to take it back straight away. After 30 seconds, other players nearby can see it and pick it up. It disappears after 3 minutes.
  • The server decides who gets each item, so nothing is ever picked up twice.

Playing together#

Everyone in your world shares the Hold. You see each other walk, fish, chop and chat in real time, and you can team up for the Depths.

Chat. Tap the chat button (or press T) to talk to everyone on the same map. Start a message with /p to talk to your party only. Messages appear above the speaker's head. Chat is filtered and rate-limited to stop spam, and it starts fresh each time you sign in: nothing is stored.

World feed. Big moments scroll across the top of the screen for everyone: rare finds, boss kills, new combat levels and deep floors reached.

Social (top right)

  • Friends: add players by hero name, accept requests, see who's online and where, and join a friend's world.
  • Party: up to 4 players. The leader opens the Delve Gate and everyone is invited into the same dungeon. Leaders can pass on leadership or remove members.
  • Nearby: everyone on your map, with quick Add friend and Invite buttons.
  • Worlds: every world and how many players are in it. Switch to one with room, or open a new one.

Party dungeons. The whole party shares one dungeon. If the leader leaves, another member takes over without a break. Loot drops for everyone, and everyone who hits a monster gets its full XP.

Name tags. Turn on Settings → Player names to see names and combat levels over everyone's heads. The colour of the level compares them to you:

ColourTheir combat level

Yellow1 to 5 levels higher
Red6 or more levels higher

Admins have a gold crown before their name, and their names always show. Lantern and Kush are the admins in Season 0.

One device at a time. Signing in on a second device signs you out on the first.

Your account and staying safe#

Your hero saves to your account automatically, so you can pick up where you left off on any device by signing in the same way.

  • Saving: your progress, settings, goals and Bank save to the server as you play. The server checks every save, so nobody can give themselves impossible gold, XP or items.
  • One hero per account, and one device online at a time.
  • Google players can connect a wallet later from the Hero screen. Anyone can link an X account to show their @handle.
  • Switching wallets in your wallet app signs you out, so you never play on the wrong account.

Staying safe

  • The only official site is lanternhold.fun. Always check the address bar.
  • Wallet sign-in is a standard Sign-In with Ethereum message that names lanternhold.fun, with a one-time code that expires after 5 minutes. Your wallet should show "lanternhold.fun" with no warning.
  • Lanternhold will never ask you for a transaction, a token approval, your seed phrase or your private key. If anything does, it isn't us. Close it and don't sign.
  • Admins will never ask for your wallet details, and never sell items for payment outside the game.

FAQ#

What do I lose if I fall in the Depths?

About half of this delve's loot and half of your bag. Your Bank and the gear you're wearing are always safe.

How do I get more oil?

Grow Lampseed at the Farm (Farming 3) and press it at the Oil Press, or buy Oil Flasks from Tallow.

How do I get more farm plots?

Level up Farming (new plots open at levels 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60) or buy them with gold from the Farm screen.

Why can't I see my friend?

You might be in different worlds. Use Social → Friends → Join world, or pick their world from Social → Worlds.

Why can't I see anyone's name?

Player names are off by default. Turn them on in Settings → Player names.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Lanternhold is built for phones first, upright or sideways.

Does signing in cost anything?

No. Wallet sign-in is a free signature, not a transaction.

Is my progress saved if I close the game?

Yes. Your hero saves to your account as you play, and crops keep growing while you're away.
